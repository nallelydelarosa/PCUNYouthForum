About the Forum

Youth leadership is already shaping the future

Young leaders around the world are advancing new approaches to environmental sustainability, mental health, conflict-related displacement, artificial intelligence, racial equity, gender and LGBTQI+ rights, education, and other urgent challenges.

The PCUN Youth Forum will create space for young people to share what motivates their work, strengthen connections across communities, and explore how psychology can support meaningful and sustainable collective action.

This year’s forum is organized by the SPSSI United Nations NGO Team in collaboration with the Psychology Coalition at the United Nations.
