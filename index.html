About the forum

Created for youth, powered by community.

The PCUN Youth Forum brings together young people who care about civic engagement, worker justice, immigrant rights, education access, and the future of Oregon communities. Participants will learn from organizers, practice leadership skills, and collaborate on ideas that can move from conversation to action.

Expect interactive workshops, keynote conversations, peer networking, and opportunities to share creative work, campaigns, or community projects. This site will be updated as final dates, speakers, and partner details are confirmed.

Who's eligible

Open to young people ready to learn, lead, and participate.

Youth participants

Designed for high school, college-age, and young adult participants who want to deepen their community leadership.

Community connection

Priority may be given to youth connected to PCUN, farmworker families, immigrant communities, or partner organizations.

All experience levels

No previous organizing experience is required. Curiosity, respect, and willingness to participate are welcome.
